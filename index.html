06 // Intelligence Database

HOI4 Glossary

Search common terminology used by HOI4 players.

Division

A military formation made from battalions and support companies.

National Focus

A country-specific objective represented through a focus tree.

Political Power

A resource used for many political and governmental actions.

Battle Plan

A planned military operation assigned to an army.

Supply

The logistical network needed to keep armies operating.

World Tension

A measure of global instability and international conflict.

Convoy

Ships used to transport resources, supplies and troops.

Division Template

The organization and composition of a division.

Air Superiority

Control of the skies over an air region.

War Support

A measure of support for a nation's war effort.
