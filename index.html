What is HOI4?

Grand strategy on a global scale.

🌍 The World

Hearts of Iron IV is a grand strategy game set around the Second World War. Players can control countries, develop industry, research technologies, organize armies, conduct diplomacy and attempt to shape the course of history.

⚔️ Your Nation

Every nation has different resources, geography, political situations and strategic possibilities. You can attempt to follow history — or create an entirely different world.

Game Systems

Click a category to learn more.

⚔️ Warfare +

Organize divisions, assign generals, create battle plans, manage front lines and coordinate land, air and naval forces.

🏭 Industry +

Build civilian and military factories, produce equipment, construct infrastructure and prepare your economy for war.

🔬 Research +

Research weapons, industry, electronics, doctrines and other technologies to improve your country's capabilities.

🏛️ Politics +

Political power can be used for decisions, advisors, laws and other changes that shape your country's direction.

✈️ Air Power +

Build aircraft and assign air wings to missions such as air superiority, interception, bombing and close air support.

⚓ Naval Warfare +

Design fleets, protect convoys, raid enemy shipping and contest important sea zones around the world.

HOI4 Glossary

Search common terms.

Division

A military formation made up of battalions and support companies.

National Focus

A major country-specific decision represented through a focus tree.

Political Power

A resource used for various political and governmental actions.

Battle Plan

A planned military operation assigned to an army.

Convoy

Ships used to transport resources, troops and supplies across oceans.

Supply

The logistical network needed to keep armies operating effectively.

World Tension

A global measure representing the escalation of international conflict.

Division Template

The structure defining which battalions and support companies a division contains.
